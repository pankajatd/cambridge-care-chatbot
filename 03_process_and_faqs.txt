3-STEP ONBOARDING PROCESS:

STEP 1: INITIAL CONSULTATION
- An unhurried phone conversation or email query with the care management team.
- Discussion of immediate needs, budget/funding considerations, and scheduling goals.
- No obligation or pushy sales tactics.

STEP 2: FREE HOME CARE ASSESSMENT
- An in-person visit by a Care Manager to the client's home in Cambridge/South Cambs.
- Detailed assessment of physical mobility, medical history, care preferences, daily routines, and home safety hazards.
- Consultation with family members and health professionals (if applicable).

STEP 3: PERSONALIZED CARE PLAN & CARER MATCHING
- A formal Care Plan is drafted and agreed upon by the family.
- Carers are carefully matched based on qualifications, personality traits, shared interests, and language preferences.
- Scheduled care visits begin, with regular 30-day, 60-day, and quarterly reviews.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS):

Q: How quickly can care start?
A: Urgent care or post-hospital discharge support can often be arranged within 24 to 48 hours following a completed assessment.

Q: Are your carers background checked?
A: Yes. Every staff member undergoes an Enhanced DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) check, full employment history verification, and minimum 2 professional reference checks prior to placement.

Q: How is care funded?
A: Care can be funded through Direct Payments (Local Authority social care funding), Personal Health Budgets (NHS Continuing Healthcare), or privately paid directly by the client or family.

Q: What happens if our regular carer is sick or on leave?
A: Cambridge Care Services provides guaranteed replacement cover using an familiar backup carer who has been briefed on the client's Care Plan.
