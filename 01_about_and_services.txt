PHILOSOPHY OF CARE:
- Person-Centered Approach: Every individual receives a bespoke care plan created through collaboration with the client and family.
- No Forced Routines: Visits are unhurried and structured around the client's preferred wake-up times, meal habits, and lifestyle preferences.
- Familiar Carers: Care teams are kept small so clients see consistent, familiar faces rather than rotating strangers.

DETAILED CARE SERVICES OFFERED:

1. PERSONAL CARE:
- Assistance with daily morning, afternoon, and evening routines.
- Washing, bathing, showering, and personal hygiene care.
- Dressing, grooming, and oral hygiene.
- Continence management, toilet assistance, and catheter/stoma care support.
- Assistance with mobility, transfer ring, and safe positioning.
- Prompting or assisting with prescribed medication schedules.

2. LIVE-IN CARE:
- A full-time, dedicated carer living in the home to provide 24/7 peace of mind.
- A direct alternative to residential care homes, allowing clients to stay in their familiar environment.
- Covers meal preparation, housekeeping, companionship, night monitoring, and personal care.
- Tailored for individuals with progressive conditions, mobility challenges, or high support needs.

3. DEMENTIA CARE:
- Specialist support for individuals living with Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia, Lewy body dementia, or frontotemporal dementia.
- Focus on maintaining familiar home routines, promoting orientation, and providing gentle reassurance.
- De-escalation strategies for agitation, anxiety, memory loss, or nocturnal confusion.
- Stimulation activities, cognitive prompts, and safe engagement.

4. COMPLEX CARE & POST-HOSPITAL DISCHARGE:
- Care for high-dependency needs, severe physical disabilities, stroke recovery, or progressive neurological conditions.
- Reablement support following hospital discharge to prevent readmission.
- Package-specific staff training tailored to the individual’s clinical and physical management plan.
- Collaboration with local District Nurses, Occupational Therapists (OTs), and GPs.

5. RESPITE CARE:
- Short-term or temporary home care cover for primary family carers.
- Can range from a few hours a week to several weeks of live-in cover while family members go on holiday or rest.
- Prevents caregiver burnout while maintaining continuity for the care recipient.

6. NIGHT CARE:
- Waking Night Support: Carers stay awake throughout the night to assist with frequent transfers, medication, repositioning, or toilet visits.
- Sleeping Night Support: Carers sleep in a spare room and are available up to 2 times during the night for unexpected needs or reassurance.

7. COMPANIONSHIP CARE:
- Social interaction, emotional support, and friendly conversation.
- Accompanied visits to local shops, cafes, doctors, or community groups in Cambridge.
- Assistance with administrative tasks, reading mail, and maintaining hobbies.

8. SUPPORTED LIVING & INDEPENDENCE:
- Practical support for adults with learning disabilities, physical disabilities, or mental health needs.
- Encourages life skill development including cooking, budget management, and community access.

9. END-OF-LIFE (PALLIATIVE) CARE:
- Compassionate, dignified support focusing on comfort, pain management assistance, and emotional care.
- Support for both the individual and their family members during the final stages of life.
