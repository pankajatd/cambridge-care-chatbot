DOCUMENT: CAREERS, RECRUITMENT & STAFF REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

JOB ROLES REGULARLY RECRUITED:
1. Domiciliary Care Assistant / Home Carer
2. Live-In Carer (Full-time resident support)
3. Night Care Assistant (Waking Night & Sleeping Night)
4. Senior Care Assistant / Field Care Supervisor
5. Complex Support Worker

MINIMUM ENTRY REQUIREMENTS FOR APPLICANTS:
- Right to Work in the UK: Must possess valid UK work authorization (settled status, valid visa, or citizenship).
- Background Check: Must undergo and pass an Enhanced DBS check.
- Driving Status: A full UK driving license and access to a reliable vehicle is preferred for community visits, though walking/cycling routes exist for central Cambridge roles.
- Experience Level: Previous NVQ / QCF Level 2 or 3 in Health and Social Care is desirable, but unexperienced candidates with strong empathy and dedication are considered as full induction training is provided.

STAFF BENEFITS & PERKS:
- Competitive hourly pay rates with higher weekend and Bank Holiday rates.
- Paid mileage allowance for travel between client homes.
- Free comprehensive Care Certificate induction training and continuous professional development (CPD).
- Flexible working hours (full-time, part-time, weekend-only, or evening shifts available).
- Ongoing mentorship, shadow shifts with senior carers, and 24/7 staff emergency helpline support.

HOW TO APPLY:
Applicants can apply directly on the Cambridge Care Services website careers page, email their updated CV to info@cambridgecareservices.co.uk, or contact recruitment directly on 07917 611 425.
