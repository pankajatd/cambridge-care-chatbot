3. DETAILED FUNDING PATHWAYS FOR HOME CARE:
- Cambridgeshire County Council Funding: Families can request a free Care Needs Assessment and Financial Assessment from Cambridgeshire County Council. Council funding may cover partial or full care costs.
- Direct Payments & Personal Budgets: Clients eligible for council support can receive direct payments to choose Cambridge Care Services directly rather than council-allocated providers.
- NHS Continuing Healthcare (CHC): For individuals with primarily health/clinical needs (e.g. advanced dementia, stroke recovery, palliative care), the NHS covers 100% of care costs regardless of personal savings.
- Attendance Allowance & State Benefits:
  * Attendance Allowance: A non-means-tested UK benefit for individuals over State Pension age who require help with personal care. Does not depend on savings.
  * Carer's Allowance: Financial support for family members providing substantial unpaid care.
- Independent Advice: Families are encouraged to consult independent advisory bodies including Age UK, Citizens Advice, or accredited later-life financial advisers.
